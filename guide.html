Installing the app

The site is installable as a PWA. Look for an Install icon in your browser's address bar, or Add to Home Screen on mobile, to pin it like a native app with an icon, offline access to pages you've already visited, and faster loads from cached audio and covers.

Achievements

The Stats page tracks 36 achievements across quiz plays, member mastery, group completion, and streaks, plus a play streak counter and a 26-week activity heatmap.

Bubudle stats

Open the stats button on Bubudle to see your win percentage, current and max streak, a guess distribution histogram, and a countdown to the next daily.
